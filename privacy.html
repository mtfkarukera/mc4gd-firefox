Privacy Policy – Magic Clipper for Google Drive

Last updated: October 2026

1. No Data Collection

Magic Clipper for Google Drive does not collect, track, store, or transmit any personal data, telemetry, or browsing activity to external or third-party servers. All processing and request execution occur locally within your browser.

2. Google Drive Access & Scope Justification

The extension requests OAuth 2.0 access to your Google Drive via the specific https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope.

Scope Usage: This permission is used solely to locate or create a single dedicated folder named "Imports Magic Clipper" in your Google Drive and upload the files, documents, or webpage captures you explicitly choose to send. It grants access only to the files and folders created or opened by Magic Clipper. The extension has zero access to any of your other personal Google Drive files, folders, or documents.

3. Local Storage

Your OAuth2 access token, token expiration timestamp, cached Google Drive folder ID, and interface language preferences are stored locally on your device via browser.storage.local. This data never leaves your device except when authenticating directly with official Google APIs.

4. Cookies and Tracking

This extension does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, analytics, or any tracking tools.

5. Contact & Open Source

This extension is fully open source under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0). For any privacy inquiries or support, contact us at contact@mtfk.fr.
